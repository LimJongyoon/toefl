I understand Ethan's point that many friends give us more opportunities. However, I believe that having a few close friends is better.

Olivia mentioned trust, but I think an even more important benefit is that it reduces stress. This is because when people feel less stress, they can focus better and stay healthy. Less stress leads to better health, and better health helps people do well in their studies and work.
