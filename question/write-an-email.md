# Write an Email (이메일)

Writing → **Write an Email** 선택 후 붙여넣기. 시험 문제 화면을 그대로 복사해도 됨.

## 규칙

- 처음 문단 = 상황 설명
- `Write an email to ...` / `In your email, do the following:` 줄 = 지시문
- 지시문 다음 줄들 = 해야 할 일 (보통 3개, 한 줄에 하나)
- `To:` 와 `Subject:` 줄은 따로 읽음. 없어도 됨
  - `To:` 가 없으면 `Write an email to Ms. Lee` 에서 받는 사람을 찾아 채움
  - `Subject:` 가 없으면 시험 중에 직접 쓰는 칸이 생김
- `Directions:` `You will have 7 minutes` 같은 안내 문장은 자동으로 버림
- 인식이 틀리면 **직접 입력 · 인식 결과 수정**을 펼쳐서 고치면 됨

## 예시

```
You recently attended a workshop organized by your city's community center. The workshop was about managing personal finances, and you found it very helpful. However, the room was too small for the number of people who attended.

Write an email to Ms. Lee, the director of the community center. In your email, do the following:
- Thank her for organizing the workshop.
- Describe something useful you learned.
- Suggest a way to improve future workshops.

To: Ms. Lee
Subject: Feedback on the finance workshop
```
