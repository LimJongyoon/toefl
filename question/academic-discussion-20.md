## 1
```
Your professor is teaching a class on education. Write a post responding to the professor's question.

Dr. Diaz
Many universities now encourage students to complete an internship before they graduate, but only some require it. Supporters say internships prepare students for real jobs, while critics say they take time away from academic study. Should universities require all students to complete an internship before graduation? Why or why not?

Claire
I think internships should be required. Classes teach theory, but students only learn how a workplace really works by being in one. Many of my friends said their internship helped them decide what career they actually wanted.

Paul
I understand Claire's point, but requiring internships is unfair to students who already work part-time to pay for school. Also, some majors, like philosophy or mathematics, do not have obvious internship options. It should be encouraged, not required.
```

Your professor is teaching a class on environmental science. Write a post responding to the professor's question.

Dr. Kim
To reduce environmental damage, some people focus on individual actions, such as recycling or using less plastic. Others argue that only large changes, like government regulations on companies, can make a real difference. Which approach do you think is more effective for protecting the environment, and why?

Andrew
I believe government regulation is much more effective. A single factory can produce more pollution in a day than thousands of people produce in a year. Without strict rules for companies, individual recycling will not change much.

Kelly
I partly agree with Andrew, but I think individual actions matter too. When many consumers change their habits, companies change their products to keep customers. Also, governments usually pass new laws only after the public starts to care.

Dr. Patel
Several companies around the world have experimented with a four-day work week, in which employees work fewer days for the same salary. Some companies reported higher productivity, while others found it difficult to serve customers. Do you think a four-day work week is a good idea for most companies? Explain your view.

Mia
I think it is a great idea. When people have more time to rest, they come back to work with more energy and focus. Studies I have read show that workers often waste time in long weeks anyway, so a shorter week can be just as productive.

Jason
It might work for office jobs, but not for every business. Hospitals, restaurants, and stores need staff every day. If they switched to a four-day week, they would need to hire more workers, which could raise prices for customers.
