# Academic Discussion (토론 글쓰기)

Writing → **Academic Discussion** 선택 후 붙여넣기. 시험 문제 화면을 그대로 복사해도 됨.

## 규칙

- `Your professor is teaching a class on ___.` 문장에서 수업 주제를 읽음
- 그다음 **교수 → 학생 1 → 학생 2** 순서
- 이름은 **혼자 한 줄**에 쓰거나 `이름: 내용` 꼴로
- 맨 마지막 두 사람이 학생, 그 앞은 전부 교수 질문으로 합침
- `In your response, you should...` `An effective response...` 같은 안내 문장은 자동으로 버림
- 인식이 틀리면 **직접 입력 · 인식 결과 수정**을 펼쳐서 고치면 됨

## 예시

```
Your professor is teaching a class on sociology. Write a post responding to the professor's question.

Dr. Diaz
Over the past few decades, more people have started working from home. Some believe this is good for society, while others think it creates new problems. In your opinion, is the growth of remote work mostly positive or negative for society? Why?

Claire
I think remote work is mostly positive. People save hours every week by not commuting, and they can spend that time with their families or on their health.

Paul
I see Claire's point, but I'm worried about the loss of social connection. Many people make friends at work, and working from home can make people feel isolated.
```

`이름: 내용` 꼴도 됨:

```
Your professor is teaching a class on economics.

Dr. Kim: Should governments tax sugary drinks? Why or why not?
Andrew: Yes. A tax would reduce consumption and help public health.
Kelly: I disagree. It mostly hurts low-income families without changing habits.
```
