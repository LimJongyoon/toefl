# Take an Interview (인터뷰)

Speaking → **Take an Interview** 선택 후 붙여넣기.

## 규칙

- **한 줄 = 질문 하나** (한 줄 안에 여러 문장이어도 한 질문으로 읽음)
- 질문 앞의 `1.` `Q1:` `Question 2.` `Interviewer:` 같은 표시는 자동으로 지움
- 답변 시간: 비워두면 **45초**, 숫자를 넣으면 그 시간으로
- 준비 시간 없이 질문이 끝나면 바로 녹음 시작 (보통 4문항)

## 예시

```
Thank you for joining our study about how students use technology. First, can you tell me about a device you use every day and what you use it for?
Some people say that using phones in class distracts students. Do you agree? Why or why not?
Think about a time when technology helped you learn something new. What happened?
In the future, do you think students will still need to attend classes in person? Explain your opinion.
```
