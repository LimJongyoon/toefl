# Write an Email — 20문제

Writing → Write an Email 선택 후, 코드 블록 하나를 통째로 붙여넣으세요.

## 1
```
You have been a member of a local gym for two years. Last week, the gym announced that it will close at 8 p.m. on weekdays instead of 11 p.m. Because you work until 7 p.m., this change makes it very difficult for you to exercise.

Write an email to Mr. Park, the manager of the gym. In your email, do the following:
- Explain why the new schedule is a problem for you.
- Describe how the change might affect other members.
- Suggest a solution that could work for both the gym and its members.

To: Mr. Park
Subject: Concern about the new closing time
```

## 2
```
You borrowed a book from the university library two weeks ago. While you were reading it on the bus, it was damaged by rain, and several pages are now difficult to read. The book is due back in three days.

Write an email to Ms. Johnson, the head librarian. In your email, do the following:
- Explain what happened to the book.
- Apologize for the damage.
- Ask what you should do to fix the situation.

To: Ms. Johnson
Subject: Damaged library book
```

## 3
```
You live in an apartment building. For the past month, your upstairs neighbors have been playing loud music late at night, often after midnight. You have an important exam next week and have not been able to sleep well.

Write an email to Mr. Lewis, the building manager. In your email, do the following:
- Describe the problem.
- Explain how it is affecting you.
- Ask him to take a specific action.

To: Mr. Lewis
Subject: Noise problem in the building
```

## 4
```
You are taking a biology course at your university. Last Thursday you were sick and missed an important lab session. Your professor said at the beginning of the semester that lab sessions cannot usually be made up.

Write an email to Dr. Martin, your biology professor. In your email, do the following:
- Explain why you missed the lab session.
- Ask whether there is any way to make up the work.
- Explain what you have already done to catch up.

To: Dr. Martin
Subject: Missed lab session last Thursday
```

## 5
```
You recently ordered a winter jacket from an online store. When the package arrived, you found that the jacket was the wrong color and two sizes too large. You need the jacket for a trip in two weeks.

Write an email to the customer service team. In your email, do the following:
- Describe the problem with your order.
- Explain why you need the correct item soon.
- Ask what options are available to you.

To: Customer Service Team
Subject: Wrong item received
```

## 6
```
Your friend Daniel, who lives in another country, is planning to visit your city next month for four days. He has never been to your city before and has asked you for advice.

Write an email to Daniel. In your email, do the following:
- Recommend one place he should visit and explain why.
- Suggest a local food he should try.
- Give him one piece of practical advice for getting around the city.

To: Daniel
Subject: Your trip next month
```

## 7
```
You signed up for an eight-week cooking class at a community center. After attending two classes, you were offered a part-time job, and the work schedule conflicts with the class time.

Write an email to Ms. Rivera, the class organizer. In your email, do the following:
- Explain why you can no longer attend the class.
- Describe what you enjoyed about the classes you attended.
- Ask whether you can receive a refund or move to a different class time.

To: Ms. Rivera
Subject: Schedule change for cooking class
```

## 8

## 9

## 10
```
You stayed at a hotel last weekend while attending a conference. After you returned home, you realized that you had left your laptop charger and a notebook with important notes in your room.

Write an email to the front desk of the hotel. In your email, do the following:

## 11
```
Many students at your university eat lunch at the campus cafeteria every day. However, most of the meals are fried or high in sugar, and there are very few vegetarian options. Some of your classmates have complained about this.

Write an email to Mr. Brown, the director of dining services. In your email, do the following:
- Describe the current problem with the menu.
- Explain why healthier options are important for students.
- Suggest one or two specific changes.

To: Mr. Brown
Subject: Suggestions for the cafeteria menu
```

## 12
```
You live in a university dormitory. The heater in your room stopped working four days ago, and the temperature at night has been very cold. You reported the problem at the front desk, but no one has come to fix it.

Write an email to Ms. Nguyen, the housing office manager. In your email, do the following:
- Describe the problem and how long it has continued.
- Explain what you have already done about it.

To: Ms. Nguyen
Subject: Broken heater in my room
```

## 13
```

Write an email to Professor Allen. In your email, do the following:

- Explain why you are applying to the program.
- Ask whether the professor would be willing to write a recommendation.

To: Professor Allen
Subject: Request for a recommendation letter
```

## 14
```

Write an email to the city parks department. In your email, do the following:

- Explain how it affects people in the neighborhood.

```

## 15
```

Write an email to Ms. Taylor, the bookstore owner. In your email, do the following:
