# Take an Interview — 20세트 (세트당 4문항)

Speaking → Take an Interview 선택 후, 코드 블록 하나를 통째로 붙여넣으세요.

## 1 — 기술
```
Thank you for joining our study about technology in daily life. First, what piece of technology do you use most often, and what do you use it for?
Some people say smartphones make us less social. Do you agree or disagree? Why?
Tell me about a time when technology caused a problem for you. What happened, and how did you solve it?
In the future, do you think people will spend more or less time using screens? Explain your opinion.
```

## 2 — 여행
```
Thanks for taking part in our research on travel habits. To start, can you tell me about a place you visited that you really enjoyed?
When you travel, do you prefer to plan everything in advance or decide things as you go? Why?
Some people think traveling alone is better than traveling with others. What is your opinion?
Do you think it is important for young people to travel to other countries? Why or why not?
```

## 3 — 음식
```
We are interested in how people make decisions about food. First, what kind of food do you usually eat for lunch on a weekday?
Do you prefer cooking at home or eating at restaurants? Explain why.
Many schools now teach students about nutrition. Do you think this is useful? Why or why not?
Some people believe that fast food should be taxed more heavily. Do you agree with this idea? Explain your view.
```

## 4 — 운동
```
Thank you for joining our study on health and exercise. Can you describe what you usually do to stay active?
Do you prefer exercising alone or with other people? Why?
Think about a time when you tried a new sport or physical activity. How did it go?
Some companies give employees time during the workday to exercise. Do you think this is a good idea? Explain.
```

## 5 — 독서
```
We are conducting research about reading habits. First, tell me about a book or article you read recently and why you chose it.
Do you prefer reading paper books or reading on a screen? Why?
Some people say young people today read less than in the past. Do you agree? Why or why not?
Should schools require students to read a certain number of books each year? Explain your opinion.
```

## 6 — 음악
```
Thanks for participating in our study about music. To begin, what kind of music do you listen to most often, and when do you usually listen to it?
Do you think learning a musical instrument is important for children? Why or why not?
Tell me about a concert or musical performance that you remember well.
Some people believe music should be free to download. What is your opinion on this?
```

## 7 — 일과 직업
```
We are studying how people think about their careers. First, can you describe a job you have had or would like to have?
What do you think is more important in a job: a high salary or interesting work? Why?
Tell me about a skill you learned from work or school that you still use today.
Do you think people in the future will change jobs more often than people do now? Explain your view.
```

## 8 — 도시 생활
```
Thank you for joining our research on city life. First, tell me about the area where you live now. What do you like about it?
Would you rather live in a big city or a small town? Why?
What is one thing your city could do to make life better for its residents?
Some people think cities should have fewer cars and more parks. Do you agree? Explain your opinion.
```

## 9 — 환경
```
We are interested in people's views on the environment. To start, what is one thing you do in your daily life to help the environment?
Do you think individuals or governments have a bigger responsibility to protect the environment? Why?
Tell me about a time when you noticed an environmental problem in your community.
Should companies be required to use only recyclable packaging? Explain your view.
```

## 10 — 우정
```
Thanks for participating in our study about friendship. First, can you tell me about a close friend and how you met?
Do you think it is easier to make friends as a child or as an adult? Why?
What is the most important quality in a good friend? Explain your answer.
Some people say online friendships can be as strong as real-life friendships. Do you agree or disagree? Why?
```

## 11 — 돈과 저축
```
We are conducting a study about money habits. First, how do you usually decide whether to buy something expensive?
Do you think it is better to save money or to spend it on experiences? Why?
Tell me about something you saved money for. Was it worth it?
Should schools teach students how to manage money? Explain your opinion.
```

## 12 — 외국어 학습
```
Thank you for joining our research on language learning. First, can you tell me about a language you have studied and why you started learning it?
What do you think is the most difficult part of learning a new language? Why?
Describe a method that helped you improve your language skills.
Some people think everyone should learn at least two foreign languages. Do you agree? Explain.
```

## 13 — 온라인 쇼핑
```
We are studying how people shop. To begin, what was the last thing you bought online, and why did you buy it online?
Do you prefer shopping online or in physical stores? Why?
Tell me about a time when you had a problem with something you bought.
Some people worry that online shopping is hurting small local businesses. What is your opinion?
```

## 14 — 여가 시간
```
Thanks for taking part in our study about free time. First, can you describe what you usually do on the weekend?
Do you prefer relaxing at home or going out in your free time? Why?
Tell me about a hobby that you started recently or would like to start.
Some people believe that people today have less free time than in the past. Do you agree? Explain.
```

## 15 — 대중교통
```
We are conducting research about transportation. First, how do you usually get to school or work, and how long does it take?
What are the advantages and disadvantages of using public transportation in your city?
Tell me about a memorable experience you had while traveling by bus, train, or subway.
Should cities spend more money on public transportation or on building new roads? Explain your view.
```

## 16 — 봉사활동
```
Thank you for joining our study about volunteering. First, have you ever volunteered for an organization or event? Tell me about it.
Why do you think some people enjoy volunteering?
Describe a way that people in your community help each other.
Some schools require students to volunteer to graduate. Do you think this is a good policy? Why or why not?
```

## 17 — 수면과 생활습관
```
We are interested in people's daily routines. To start, can you describe your typical morning routine?
Do you consider yourself a morning person or a night person? Why?
Tell me about a habit you would like to change and why.
Some people think schools should start later in the morning so students can sleep more. Do you agree? Explain.
```

## 18 — 영화와 엔터테인먼트
```
Thanks for participating in our research on entertainment. First, what kind of movies or shows do you enjoy watching?
Do you prefer watching movies at a theater or at home? Why?
Tell me about a movie or show that changed the way you think about something.
Some people say that watching too much TV is a waste of time. What is your opinion?
```

## 19 — 명절과 전통
```
We are studying how people celebrate holidays. First, tell me about a holiday that is important in your country or family.
How do you usually spend that holiday, and who do you spend it with?
Do you think traditional holidays are becoming less important today? Why or why not?
Is it important for young people to learn about their culture's traditions? Explain your opinion.
```

## 20 — 온라인 수업
```
Thank you for joining our study about education. First, have you ever taken an online class? How was the experience?
What are the main differences between learning online and learning in a classroom?
Tell me about a teacher who had a strong influence on you.
In the future, do you think most university classes will be taught online? Explain your view.
```
